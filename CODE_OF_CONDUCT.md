# Code of conduct

We want PR Sift to be a friendly place to contribute, whatever your experience, language or background.

## Expected

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Give specific, kind feedback on code and ideas, not on people.
- Accept that maintainers decide what fits the project, and explain disagreement calmly.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language or jokes.
- Publishing someone's private information.
- Spam or repeated off-topic posts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, issues and pull requests, and block people who break these rules. Report a
problem by contacting a maintainer privately through the repository.
